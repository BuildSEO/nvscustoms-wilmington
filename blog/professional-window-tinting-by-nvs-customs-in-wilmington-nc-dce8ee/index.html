Cutting-Edge Window Film Technology NVS Customs offers two main types of window tint: ceramic and carbon film. Each of these has distinct advantages depending on your needs and preferences. Ceramic window tint is highly regarded because of its ability to reject up to 96% of infrared heat. This high level of infrared rejection translates to increased comfort for everyone inside the vehicle, especially during the hot summers in the Wilmington area. Carbon window tint is another excellent option, delivering 34% infrared rejection.
