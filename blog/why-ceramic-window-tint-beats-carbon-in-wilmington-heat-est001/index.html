Why Ceramic Window Tint Beats Carbon in Wilmington Heat

Nelson LopezNVS Customs

Wilmington summers push window film to its limit, and the difference between ceramic and carbon tint shows up fastest in how much heat actually gets through.

Ceramic window tint rejects up to 96 percent of infrared heat, compared to 34 percent for carbon film. That gap matters most in a parked car in direct sun, where cabin temperature climbs fastest through the glass.
